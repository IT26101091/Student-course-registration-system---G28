Registration Guidelines

Important semester enrollment policies

Credit Load Limits Students are recommended to take 12 to 20 credits per academic term. Always balance core programming modules with electives.
Course Dropping Policy You may drop an active course at any time. Dropping immediately frees up a seat and triggers automatic promotion for students waiting in the queue.
Full Capacity Courses When a course reaches maximum capacity, attempting to register will offer you an immediate spot on the waiting list.

How Waitlists Work
